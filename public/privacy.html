Powtracker

Privacy policy

In force from 18 November 2026. This covers the Powtracker website at powtracker.app and the Powtracker iPhone app.

The short version

Powtracker has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not collect, store or sell information about you. What you choose in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved mountains, units, filters and the last forecast period you looked at. They are kept in the browser's or the app's own storage and are never sent to us.
  • Your location, if you choose "Nearest to me". It is used on the device to work out distances and is not sent anywhere or stored.
  • In the iPhone app, whether a Powtracker Pro subscription is active, as confirmed by the App Store on the device.

Services your device contacts

To show forecasts and maps, your device fetches data directly from the services below. As with any website, each one sees your device's network address and the request it receives. Powtracker sends them nothing else about you.

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and its forecast files.
  • Open-Meteo, for weather model output for the mountain you open.
  • OpenFreeMap and Amazon Web Services (open terrain and weather data), for map tiles.
  • The resort's own lift and trail feed (Alterra Mountain Company or Intermaps), when you open a mountain that publishes one.
  • avalanche.org and Avalanche Canada, when you open a mountain they cover.
  • RainViewer, if you turn on the radar layer on the website.

Purchases

Powtracker Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and does not share your payment details with us. We receive no name, email address or card number. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Powtracker is a general weather app. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
