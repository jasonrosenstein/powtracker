What Powtracker is

Powtracker shows snowfall forecasts, measured snow, trail maps and resort information for ski mountains. Forecasts are estimates worked out from weather models. They will sometimes be wrong, and they get less certain the further ahead they look.

Not for safety decisions

Do not rely on Powtracker to decide whether terrain, weather, roads or avalanche conditions are safe. Lift and trail status, opening dates and avalanche ratings are shown as published by resorts and avalanche centres, and may be out of date or incomplete. Check the resort and the official avalanche forecast before you go.
