Powtracker Pro

  • Cancel or change the subscription: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Powtracker. Or open the App Store, tap your picture, then Subscriptions.
  • Pro is not unlocked after buying, or on a new phone: open Powtracker, tap "Try Pro", then "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them at reportaproblem.apple.com.

About the numbers

  • Why does the forecast differ from another app? Powtracker averages eight weather models and works out snow separately for the base, the middle and the summit. Other apps use different models and different heights.
  • Why is there no snow depth for my mountain? Depth is shown only where an official snow station stands within 10 km of the mountain and at its height.
  • Why are there no live lifts for my mountain? Live lifts and trails appear only where the resort publishes them for other sites to read. Elsewhere Powtracker links to the resort's own page.
